ABOUT

What kind of architect am I?

A career built by widening scope — from application code to enterprise platforms to autonomous systems.

My career began in application development and enterprise collaboration platforms, then moved into software and event-driven architecture, then into cloud — designing and governing Azure landing zones at enterprise scale. Over the last several years that scope has widened again, into platform engineering and, most recently, enterprise Agentic AI.

I don't think of myself as someone who deploys Azure resources. I think of myself as someone who designs how an enterprise's technology foundation should be structured — governance, security, networking, reliability — and increasingly, how autonomous AI agents fit into and accelerate that foundation.

That evolution shapes how I approach every engagement: start with the architecture and the governance model, not the tooling.
